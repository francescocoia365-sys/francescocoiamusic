“the detail of the pattern is movement”

Francesco Coia is an Italian composer and sound designer based in Europe. He writes for film, television, theatre, dance and video games, moving between orchestral writing, electronic production and experimental sound.

His work spans feature films, Netflix series, television documentaries and large-scale theatre, on Italian and international productions. Projects he has contributed to have been recognised at the Nastri d’Argento and Globi d’Oro and presented at the Venice International Film Festival.

He also works with composer Paolo Buonvino, touring year-round with his two live shows, La musica oltre le storie and Racconti di ispirazione.

He holds a degree in Jazz from the Conservatory of Pescara and a Master’s in Film Scoring from Saint Louis College of Music in Rome, both with highest honours, and has taken part in courses at Berklee College of Music.
